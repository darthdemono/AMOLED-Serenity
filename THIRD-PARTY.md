## Notes on the OFL fonts

The SIL Open Font License permits embedding, subsetting, and redistribution, including inside a
larger work under a different licence. It requires that:

1. The copyright notice and licence text ship with the font — satisfied by this file plus
   [`LICENSES/OFL-1.1.txt`](LICENSES/OFL-1.1.txt).
2. The fonts are not sold on their own. They are not; they are a component of a free theme.
3. Modified versions do not use a Reserved Font Name. Neither Bricolage Grotesque nor Montserrat
   declares a Reserved Font Name in its `name` table, so the subsets here retain the original
   family names.
